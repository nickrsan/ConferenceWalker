## 2. Technical Architecture & Decisions

### Resizable Sidebar Architecture
- Dedicated draggable `#sidebar-resizer` handle on the right edge of the desktop sidebar with `role="separator"`, `aria-orientation="vertical"`, `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, and `aria-valuemax`.
- Pointer capture (`setPointerCapture`) and `requestAnimationFrame` update loops for smooth dragging.
- Full keyboard support: `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` (adjusts by 20px), `Home` (snaps to 300px min), `End` (snaps to 700px max).
- Automatically hidden on mobile viewports (`<= 768px`) where mobile drawer behavior applies.
- Dispatches `onResize` event invoking `mapManager.resize()` to keep MapLibre canvas and viewport in sync.

### Prominent Filter Bar & Summary Tags
- Displayed below tabs in `SidebarComponent` when the filter panel is collapsed.
- Includes a prominent `🎛️ Edit Filters` CTA button and quick tags indicating active categories, walk time limits, nearest-only status, open-now status, cuisines, and data source filters.
- Clicking any tag or the edit button expands the filter panel.

### Isochrone Layer Visibility & State Sync
- Added `setIsochronesVisible(visible: boolean)` to `MapManager` updating MapLibre layout visibility for `isochrones-fill`, `isochrones-line`, and `isochrones-labels`.
- Re-applies isochrone visibility state across basemap theme changes.
- Added toggle in the Settings drawer with `localStorage` persistence (`cw_show_isochrones`).
- Synchronized via URL query parameters (`?isochrones=false` and `?isochrones=true`).

### Configuration & Branding
- Extended `ConferenceWalkerConfig` in `src/config.ts` with `conferenceSubtitle`, `conferencePinLabel`, `conferencePinTitle`, `welcomeHtml`, and `showIsochronesByDefault`.
- Displayed `APP_CONFIG.conferenceSubtitle` (falling back to `region.name.split(',')[0]`) in the sidebar header.
- Displayed `APP_CONFIG.conferencePinLabel` and title on the on-map center pin.
- Rendered `APP_CONFIG.welcomeHtml` in a dismissible card at the top of the sidebar (`cw_welcome_dismissed`).
